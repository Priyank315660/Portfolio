Bin 04

How I work on an edit

  1. Step 1

    Ingest

    Sync, rename and sort footage. Create proxies and a bin structure before the first cut.

  2. Step 2

    Assembly

    Lay out the best takes in story order, and match the structure to the brief.

  3. Step 3

    Rough cut

    Trim for pace, remove dead air, and test the opening hook on its own.

  4. Step 4

    Fine cut

    Tighten transitions, sync cuts to the music, and add titles and graphics.

  5. Step 5

    Colour and sound

    Balance shots, apply the grade, clean dialogue, and mix music and effects.

  6. Step 6

    Export and revise

    Deliver the right format for each platform, then apply one clear round of notes.
